04 / Technical innovation

From PDF clues to field context.

The useful unit is the answer attached to the right field - not a chatbot reply floating free of the form.

01

Ingest

Upload a government form and extract fields, labels, sections, and page structure. Turn a static PDF into a navigable experience.

02

Understand

Analyze the selected field alongside its surrounding instructions, labels, and form metadata. The field is understood in context, not in isolation.

03

Guide

Generate plain-language explanations and identify the information required to complete the selected field. Users understand what the form is asking for and why.

04

Discover

Recommend documents, records, and official resources that contain the required information. Help users locate the information instead of guessing.

NOW

Ingestion: Browser-side PDF.js renders the PDF and extracts its fillable fields.

Understand: Curated fro rules supply known field guidance, with text based heuristics as a fallback - Rule based.

Guide: Gemini streams converstational answers throguh a Next.js API route.

Discover:The service finder performs AI/Web serach agaisnt a curated catalog.

05 / Architecture

A small pipeline.
Clear boundaries.

Toggle the blueprint to peek at the gated future plan.

01

Ingest & Render

Interactive Form Completion & Export

Next.js/React accepts PDFs and PDF.js processes them entirely in the browser. It renders each page ad overlays clickable fields using extracted PDF from metadata.

Transform FormBeacon from a guidance tool into a complete form-filling assistant. Users will be able to populate supported PDF fields directly within the platform, save their progress, and download a completed version ready for review or submission.

02

Identify & Explain

Explanding Knowledge Hub

TypeScript matches form fields to curated rules, currently inclusing the adult passport application; general heuristics cover unmatched fields. The /api/explain route returns the explanation and relevant offical links.

Users will be able to access forms, requirements, and supporting information from a single unified platform instead of navigating multiple portals.

03

Chat & Discover

Application Readiness Assistant

The Next.js /api/chat endpoint validates form metadata and sends the question and context to Gemini for a streamed response.

identify missing information, and proactively flag potential issues before submission. This helps reduce errors, increase application completeness, and improve user confidence throughout the process

PDF stays in browser

Only needed field and chat context reaches local routes.
