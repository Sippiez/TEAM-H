  2. 0:25 Passport form

0:00–0:25

Introduce the demo

SHOW

Landing page.

SAY

Hello everyone, let’s get this demo started by seeing how formER helps with a passport application.

0:25–1:05

Open a familiar example

DO

Click “Adult passport application” under “Try a sample form.”

SAY

Let’s say I’m not sure what this form means when it asks for my parent’s surname at birth. Does that mean their current name, or something different?

DO

Click “Surname of parent(s) at their birth” and let the explanation appear.

1:05–1:35

Show the explanation

SHOW

The selected field and its explanation.

SAY

I can simply click this field and an explanation pops up.

1:35–2:10

Ask a follow-up

SAY

Let’s say I’m not satisfied with the explanation. I can simply go to the Ask panel and ask a follow-up.

DO

Open the Ask panel and ask a follow-up question about the parent’s surname field.

2:10–2:35

Switch to French

DO

Switch the interface to French. Do not wait for another generated response.

SAY

And of course, we can switch to French as needed. Here, the original PDF remains unchanged.

2:35–3:20

Find the Canada Pension Plan form

SAY

Now let’s say I’m looking to download the Canada Pension Plan form, and I don’t know where to find it.

DO

Start a new chat and ask for the official download link.

SAY · WHILE LOADING

In this case, I can just start a chat and ask for the download link.

DO

Show the official page in the response and open it to download the form.

3:20–4:40

Add the form to chat

DO

Attach the downloaded form to the chat.

SAY

I can now download the form from the official page and add it to the chat for any explanations I might need.

4:40–5:00

Leave room to breathe

SHOW

Use the remaining time for delays or questions.

If a live answer fails

SAY

The live answer hasn’t completed, so I won’t present it as verified. Here’s a screenshot from our rehearsal showing this same step.

Then continue the story. Don’t debug on stage.
